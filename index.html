01 — Meet the place

What is
Magic City?

Magic City is a fun park in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. You can see a big castle, ride attractions, visit an aquarium, watch a film and eat tasty food.

Families and friends can enjoy many activities here.

02 — Where it is

In the heart
of Tashkent

Magic City is in Tashkent, the capital of Uzbekistan. Its address is 174 Babur Street.

  • Address: 174 Babur Street, Tashkent
  • You can go there by bus or car.
  • It is close to other central parts of the city.
